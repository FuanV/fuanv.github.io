Fuan Nisshoku

Abandoned and cast aside from their birth clan (Zenin Clan) for bearing a modified version of the 10 Shadows Technique. Being banished from the clan, Fuan roams the streets trying to find purpose in their actions and survive.

Fuan values their personal code of law and order above all else, morality and money comes second. They are distant but sporadic and normally prefers ambushes over direct confrontation.

Lunar Shadows Technique

If a shikigami that is not a Lunarbound shikigami is destroyed, the shikigami becomes a phantom. The shikigami loses all consciousness and will, and becomes a shell of what it once was. All abilities, stats and attacks of the phantom are disadvantaged by -2 permanently. If a phantom is destroyed, it is rendered unusable and sent into a recovery period.

Binding Vows

The user is able to summon parts of shikigami to attach to their body. The user wishes for the parts that are attached to be able to have enhanced capabilities and be able to do more damage, and speed (exert 110-120% of maximum power). In exchange the user will suppress their full shikigami to 80-90% of their maximum power and the more parts summoned equates to the more power suppressed. For example, the user having claws equates to around 120%, if the shikigami is half summoned, its around 100%, if the shikigami is fully summoned its around 80%

The user is able to break the vow and use 100% of their full shikigami in exchange for how long the dm says (e.g until the end of an encounter), partially summoned shikigami results in lower capabilities and power.
